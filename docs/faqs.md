# frequently asked questions

This list is a compilation of questions that we have received from all channels including email.

If you dont find what you are looking for inside this list send an email to support@ressonance.com. Whe will be very happy to assist you.


## Is it a Laravel Reverb?

It's a cloud implementation of reverb using the power websocket protocol. Stop restarting your server after every deployment. This means you can focus on building your application without worrying about the underlying infrastructure.

## What is the advantage compared with self hosted reverb?

To Keep a self hosted reverb you will have an engineer cost to implement and the infrastructure cost. Reverb does not work well in shared hosts too. To simple put reverb keeps the headache away.

## Then to use Ressonance i just need the Laravel Reverb Broadcast Driver?

Yes, just follow the Laravel documentation and you are good to go.

## How is it compared with Pusher?

Pusher has a ton of features but it is expensive. Most applications just need channels and push notifications. Ressonance can deliver it cheaper.

## Is Ressonance only for Laravel applications?

Ressonance is meant for Laravel Applications but Reverb is compatible with Pusher API. Then you can use Ressonance with other stacks.

## Can I use pusher libraries to integrate with Ressonance?

Yes but pusher has more features that will not work on Ressonance. Only channels will work.

## Is there a contract commitment?

No. leave whenever you want.

## What about security?

Ressonance does not record your messages for regulation ends. All communication is encrypted.

## Ressonance can help with the first steps?

Absolutely. For the paid plans we are open to help you and your team with the first steps even getting the hands dirty.
